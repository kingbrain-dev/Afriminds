Why AFRIMINDS exists

Our mission, the problem, and our solution.

01

Our mission

To turn South Sudan’s natural-resource potential into safe, accessible, and sustainable educational opportunities for young people.

02

The problem

Too many children are out of school, while rural schools often lack learning resources, digital equipment, and comfortable classroom furniture.

03

Our solution

We connect schools with partners, redistribute educational resources, and design affordable local solutions that improve learning environments.

Our initiatives

Resources that keep moving.

From sharing educational equipment to creating better classroom furniture, we are building practical solutions for underserved schools.

01

Resource-sharing campaign

Re-Source Africa

Reuse to Rise, Hope to Thrive

Re-Source Africa is an educational resource-sharing campaign that collects used books, computers, and other ICT equipment from urban schools, organizations, and institutions and redistributes them to underserved rural schools. By giving unused educational resources a second life, we aim to bridge the educational and digital divide, promote sustainability, and ensure that more students have access to the tools they need to learn, innovate, and succeed.

02

School furniture

AfriMinds Enterprise

Building Better Learning Environments

AfriMinds Enterprise designs, manufactures, and supplies affordable, durable school furniture to underserved rural schools. Our four- or six-student shared-table desk is designed for overcrowded classrooms, making the most of limited space while giving more students comfortable, functional learning spaces. By using local materials and production, we also create employment opportunities, empower communities, and support sustainable development.
