01 / Who we are

A continent rich in resources.
Abundant in human potential.

We are AFRIMINDS, a team of young African changemakers who believe South Sudan’s future is shaped in classrooms, workshops, and spaces for innovation.

South Sudan’s natural-resource wealth and its young people’s potential can go hand in hand. We want to connect them through practical, technology-driven learning that builds the knowledge and skills to create value responsibly.

02 / Our mission

Make learning a force for transformation.

We strengthen education in South Sudan through practical, technology-driven learning. Our ambition is to equip young people to responsibly develop and explore the country’s natural resources—and contribute to Africa’s future.
