Our impact model

How AFRIMINDS works.

We connect schools with partners, natural-resource businesses, and communities—and track every step from commitment to completion.

Learn more →
♧→

1. Identify needs

Schools submit their infrastructure needs and we verify them.

♟→

2. Connect partners

Natural-resource businesses, donors and investors offer support and resources.

◇→

3. Fund projects

Projects are prioritised, budgeted and matched with the right partners.

▤→

4. Build & track

Construction progress, spending and milestones are published in real time.

◆

5. Lasting impact

More classrooms, better learning and stronger communities.
